Here is the most efficient order of operations:
Phase 1: The "Data Pipeline" (Start Here)
1. Update SEO.js: Prepare the "container" that will hold your meta tags.
2. Update PostDetailPage.js: Connect your Django seo_title and seo_descriptionto the SEO.js component.
• Goal: Verify that when you click a post, the browser tab title changes to your SEO Title instead of just the blog title.

Phase 2: The "Skeleton" (Semantic HTML)
Once the metadata is flowing, fix the HTML structure so Google knows how to read that data.
1. Layout/Header/Footer: Wrap your navigation in <nav>, your branding in <header>, and your credits in <footer>.
2. Page Components: Go through Homepage, CategoryPage, and PostDetailPage to ensure you are using <main>, <article>, and correct <h1>-<h3> hierarchies.
3. Image Tagging: Update all <img> tags to use the captions/alt text from your backend.

Phase 3: The "Map" (External Files)
Now that the site structure is perfect, give the search engines the "instruction manual."
1. sitemap.xml: Create this (ideally on the Django side) so Google has a list of every URL you’ve ever created.
2. robots.txt: Create this to tell bots which folders to ignore (like /admin) and where the sitemap is located.
